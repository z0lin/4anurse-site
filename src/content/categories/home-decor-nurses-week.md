Decor is the Nurses Week category that risks becoming clutter, so the useful filter is simple: will it be used up, or will it fit somewhere that actually exists? Candles satisfy the first test. Small plaques and figurines satisfy the second, provided the recipient has a desk rather than just a locker.

## Candles do the most work

**Scented candles** are the strongest pick here, and not only because they are safe. They match something real about the job — nurses talk constantly about the ritual of getting home, showering the shift off, and sitting down. A candle plays into that in a way a wall sign does not. Lavender and soy options dominate for a reason, and most sit comfortably under $25 for a unit-wide buy.

## Plaques, figurines and the desk question

**Affirmation plaques** and **nurse figurines** carry the appreciation message more explicitly, which is why they show up so often around Nurses Week. Before buying, picture where it goes: a home office or bookshelf, almost certainly, rather than a nurses' station. Given that, these read better as a personal thank-you from a patient, family, or manager than as a bulk gift for a floor — the bulk buys are better served by the drinkware and candles.
