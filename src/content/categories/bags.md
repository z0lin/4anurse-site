A work bag is one of the few nurse gifts that gets used before the shift starts and after it ends. It also tends to be the item people put off replacing longest — most nurses are carrying whatever bag they already owned when they started the job, which is usually not built for scrubs, food, and equipment at the same time.

## Backpacks for commuters and floaters

The **LOVEVOOK** and **ETRONIK** backpacks here are built around the realities of a hospital shift: a padded laptop sleeve, a separate insulated section so lunch does not sit against clean clothes, and enough structure that the bag stands up in a locker instead of collapsing. The USB pass-through on the ETRONIK is a small thing that gets used constantly, given how much nurses depend on a charged phone. These suit anyone commuting, floating between units, or studying alongside work.

## Totes and clinical organisers

For nurses who work from a fixed base — clinic, school, telehealth desk, case management — a **tote with compartments** is easier to live with than a backpack, and the utility versions with ten or more pockets stop small equipment disappearing into the bottom. **Accordion-file clinical bags** are the right answer for home health and community nursing, where paperwork and supplies need to be found quickly in someone else's house. Several of these are large enough to double as a hospital bag or overnight bag, which makes them a reasonable gift for a travel nurse between contracts.
