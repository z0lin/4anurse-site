Finishing nursing school is no small feat. Between the brutal clinicals, NCLEX prep, and three years of sleep deprivation, your new grad has earned more than another set of scrubs. Below are our favorite gift ideas — from sentimental keepsakes to genuinely useful tools they'll carry into their first job.

## How to pick the right graduation gift

The best nursing graduation gifts fall into three buckets: **practical** (things they'll use on shift, like a quality stethoscope or compression socks), **sentimental** (jewelry, keepsakes, framed pinning ceremony photos), or **celebratory** (something fun to mark the moment that has nothing to do with work). The right choice depends on how close you are to the grad and what you know about their personality.
