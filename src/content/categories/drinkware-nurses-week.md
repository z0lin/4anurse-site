Nurses Week creates a specific problem: you often need one gift, repeated fifteen or twenty times, that nobody will feel short-changed by. Drinkware is the category that solves it best — every nurse drinks something during a shift, an insulated cup gets used daily rather than shelved, and the price band lets you cover a whole floor without a committee meeting about budget.

## Buying for a unit rather than a person

The **nutrition-facts tumblers** and insulated **travel mugs** here are the practical picks: lids that survive a bag, sizes that fit a locker, and enough visual personality to feel like a present rather than supplies. Keeping to a single design across the team reads as deliberate rather than cheap. If you want a little variation, choose one tumbler model and let colour be the difference.

For an individual rather than a group, the **funny nurse mugs** carry more personality and work well paired with good coffee or a gift card. Just check whether they actually have desk space — floor nurses rarely do, which is why the travel options win more often than they should have to.
