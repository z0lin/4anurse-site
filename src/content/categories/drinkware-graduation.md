A tumbler is the small half of a good graduation gift. New nurses spend their first year learning that hydration and caffeine are logistics problems, not preferences, and the cup they carry gets used every single shift from day one.

## Why personalisation matters here specifically

Graduation is the one moment where putting **RN** on something is not a novelty — it is the point. A new graduate has just spent years earning those two letters, and a tumbler with their name and credentials marks it in a way a generic cup does not. That is the difference between a present and a supply, and it is why the personalised and nutrition-facts designs do better as graduation gifts than plainer alternatives.

## Pair it, don't rely on it

Treat drinkware as the accompaniment. The strongest graduation gifts in our experience combine one substantial item — a **stethoscope** is the obvious candidate — with the small practical things a new nurse has not thought to buy yet: a tumbler, a badge reel, a set of reference cards. Together that reads as someone who understood the job they were walking into.
