Birthdays inside a nursing team are frequent, informal, and usually capped at whatever people will happily chip in. That makes drinkware the practical answer rather than a lazy one — it is useful, it lands under $25, and it does not require knowing anything about someone's home or wardrobe.

## The colleague birthday problem

Buying for a coworker means guessing with very little information, and drinkware fails gracefully. A **funny nurse mug** works if you know their humour; an **insulated tumbler** works if you do not. Both get used. Both are easy to hand over in a break room without ceremony. And at this price point nobody has to work out whether they now owe you a bigger gift back, which is a genuine consideration among colleagues.

## Making it feel less generic

The upgrade is context, not spend. A tumbler with good coffee beans, a mug filled with their preferred snacks, or a cup paired with a written card all turn a $20 item into something that reads as considered. For a closer friend on the team, a **personalised** design lifts it further without changing the budget much.
