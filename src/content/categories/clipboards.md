A clipboard sounds like a boring gift right up to the moment you see how much a nurse relies on one. Report sheets, brain sheets, vitals, med times, and handover notes all live on that board for twelve hours, and the difference between a $3 school clipboard and a purpose-built nursing one is real.

## Cheat sheets where you actually need them

The foldable **nursing edition** clipboards are the standout category here. They fold in half to fit a scrub pocket and have reference tables printed straight onto the board — lab values, normal ranges, EKG basics, conversions, and drip rates. That is information nurses look up several times a shift, and having it on the board rather than in a phone is faster and looks considerably more professional at the bedside. For students and new grads still building recall, this is the single most practical item on the page.

## Storage, organisation and privacy

**Storage clipboards** such as the Sooez and Tribe RN models add a compartment beneath the writing surface, which keeps printed patient information flat and out of sight — a small but real privacy benefit compared with a sheet folded into a pocket. The **padfolio and zip-pouch** versions from Ospelelf extend this into a full organiser holding pens, shears, sticky notes, and a phone, which suits charge nurses, case managers, and community nurses moving between sites more than it suits someone at a single bedside.
