Stationery is where nurse gifting works hardest per dollar. Everything on this page costs less than $20, all of it gets consumed rather than displayed, and none of it requires you to guess a size, a colour preference, or a specialty.

## Pens, markers and sticky notes

Assume any pen a nurse brings to work will be lost, and buy accordingly — **12-packs of quick-dry gel pens** are a better gift than one nice pen, and the **Sharpie minis with keychain clips** survive longest because they physically attach to a lanyard or badge reel. **Mini medical sticky notes** cover the constant business of flagging a chart, leaving a note for the next shift, or marking a page in a binder, and the nurse-specific pads add a bit of personality to an otherwise purely functional item.

## Stickers and shift notebooks

**Nurse sticker sets** in 50, 100, and 200-piece packs are the safe home for the profession's humour: a joke that would be too much on a badge in front of families is perfectly fine on the back of a laptop or a water bottle. There are Grey's Anatomy sets here too for the nurses who watch it specifically to complain about it. For anyone who prefers writing things down, the **stethoscope-embossed medical notebooks** work as shift journals, brain-sheet templates, or study notes — and unlike an app, they still work when the hospital wifi does not.
