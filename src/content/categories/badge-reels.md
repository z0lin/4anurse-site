Badge reels are the most reliable small gift in nursing, for a very unglamorous reason: they are consumables. The retraction spring gives out, the cord frays, the clip snaps, or the whole thing simply disappears somewhere between the med room and a patient's bedside. Every nurse needs a replacement several times a year and almost none of them buy one until the old one has completely failed.

## Personality on a $10 budget

A reel is one of the few pieces of a nurse's uniform they get to choose, which is why the funny ones do so well. The **dumpster fire** and **black cat "It's Fine"** designs are the ones colleagues actually comment on, and **"Sure Is Quiet"** is a running joke on any unit that believes in jinxing a shift. For nurses who prefer something softer there are felt animal sets, the **pink bow stethoscope** reel, and **highland cow** and **turtle** designs. Multipacks of three to eight reels are common, which makes them ideal for Nurses Week when you need to give something to an entire floor rather than one person.

## Pairing them into a better gift

On their own a badge reel is a nice small gesture. Combined, it becomes a real present: add a set of **nursing reference cards** for lab values and EKG rhythms, a **badge buddy** credential card, and a multipack of **penlights**, and you have assembled the everyday-carry kit most nurses never get round to buying for themselves — usually still for less than $40.
