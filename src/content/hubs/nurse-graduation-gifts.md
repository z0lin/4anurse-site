Finishing nursing school is a genuine achievement — years of clinicals, care plans, and NCLEX prep — and the right graduation gift celebrates the milestone *and* sets the new RN up for a strong start. The best nurse graduation gifts fall into two camps: **practical gear** they'll use from day one (a real stethoscope, a great badge reel, comfortable shoes) and **keepsakes** that honor the moment (engraved jewelry, a pinning gift, a framed display).

Not sure which way to go? A simple rule: if you're close family, lean toward a milestone keepsake or a career-long tool like a quality stethoscope. If you're a friend or coworker, a thoughtful, useful item in the $20–40 range is perfect.
