ICU and critical care nurses run some of the most demanding shifts in healthcare — constant monitoring, high-acuity patients, and life-or-death decisions, hour after hour. The best gifts for ICU nurses respect that reality: they're **practical** enough to survive a 12-hour shift and **comforting** enough to help with the decompression afterward.

Think reliable, every-shift gear (a cardiology-grade stethoscope, premium compression socks, an insulated tumbler that actually keeps coffee hot) paired with genuine self-care for the recovery side. Skip the gimmicky novelty items — critical care nurses appreciate things that make the job a little easier or the recovery a little kinder.
