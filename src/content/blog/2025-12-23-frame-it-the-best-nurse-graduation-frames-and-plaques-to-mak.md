Congratulations! A nursing degree is no small feat, and your favorite soon-to-be nurse deserves to be showered with love and recognition. What better way to immortalize this achievement than with a beautiful graduation frame or plaque? Whether they're hoping to jazz up their dorm or decorate their new desk, these gifts will have them gushing with gratitude!

## 1. Picture-Perfect Graduation Frames
Let's get personal! A customized photo frame is the way to go when you want to add a personal touch to your gift. Consider frames with fun nursing themes or inspirational quotes etched into them. Slide in a heartwarming photo from their pinning ceremony or graduation day for an extra special touch.

## 2. Diploma Display Frames
A frame worthy of their achievements! Nurses work tirelessly to hit that graduation milestone, and their diploma deserves to be on display. Opt for a sleek and professional diploma display frame that features sturdy materials and protective glass (because let's face it, that paper is ever-so-precious!).

## 3. A Plaque with Praise
Turn admiration into art with a personalized plaque that solidifies their status as Heroes in Scrubs. Choose plaques that allow you to add names, graduation dates, or a heartfelt message. It's a classy way to remind them daily that their hard work is cherished.

## 4. Multi-Photo Frames
Because one picture is never enough! Multi-photo frames let your nurse grad showcase a collage of memories. From study groups to celebratory dinners, those snaps deserve a spotlight on the wall. These frames allow for creative freedom and are perfect for those with a fun side!

## 5. Wall of Fame Shadow Boxes
For the ultimate display, shadow boxes are the MVPs. Capture the essence of their nursing journey by incorporating graduation cap tassels, stethoscopes, or any other mementos that mark their milestone. It's a walk down memory lane every time they glance at it.
