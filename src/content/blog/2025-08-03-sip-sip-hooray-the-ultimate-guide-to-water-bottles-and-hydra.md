## Why Hydration Gifts are the Real MVP
Water bottles are not just a thirst-quenching dream but a practical must-have for any nurse's toolkit. They keep those H2O levels topped up during grueling 12-hour shifts, and the right bottle can add a splash of personality (and sanity) to a hectic workday. Look for designs that feature motivational slogans, hourly markings, or cheeky humor that can bring a smile during tough days.

## Top Picks for Fab H2O Vessels
- **Insulated Water Bottles:** These are perfect for keeping drinks hot or cold no matter what the shift throws their way. Plus, the sleek designs can complement even the funkiest of scrubs.- **Collapsible Bottles:** Space savers! These nifty gadgets fold into a pocket-sized package when empty, making them convenient for the nurse who's always on the go.- **Infuser Bottles:** Because who doesn't want a hint of strawberry in their water? Adds a twist of flavor and a touch of elegance to everyday hydration.
