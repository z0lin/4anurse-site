Gift-giving doesn’t have to mean busting your budget, especially when it comes to showing appreciation for the superheroes in scrubs. Whether it’s for Nurse Appreciation Week, a birthday, or just because they’re awesome, you can find amazing nurse gifts under $20 that deliver joy and gratitude wrapped up with a bow.

## 1. This Socks to Be on Your Feet All Day!
Every nurse needs a cozy break, and nothing says comfort like a pair of delightful and supportive socks. Look for options with cute, nursing-themed patterns or funny sayings that keep their toes warm even during the longest night shifts.

## 2. Badges? Meet Your New Reel MVP!
Nurses wear their hearts on their sleeves and their IDs on lanyards. Elevate their badge game with a quirky badge reel featuring their favorite superhero, a witty quote, or even a shining stethoscope design. Bonus points if it’s retractable – we all love some stretch in our step!

## 3. Hydration, But Make It Stylish
Enter the tumbler, the nurse’s best friend after coffee. Seek out tumblers with funny nurse-related graphics or inspirational quotes. Double points for leak-proof designs that keep their coffee or tea just the right temp.

## 4. Sticky Notes for Savvy Scribes
Nurses juggle a million tasks at once, and sticky notes are their little helpers. Find sets adorned with cheeky nurse jokes or practical ones with tabs, so they can color-code their thoughts as strategically as they organize patient charts.
