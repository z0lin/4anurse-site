Nurses work in a world of high stress and long shifts. Treating them to monthly spa subscription boxes is like sending a personal masseuse, sans the awkward hotel robe. Nothing screams relaxation like dropping a fizzing, fragrant bath bomb into a warm bath. Whether they're into calming lavender or invigorating citrus, there's a subscription box that has them covered. Bath bombs are heaven-sent for their achy muscles and frazzled minds.
