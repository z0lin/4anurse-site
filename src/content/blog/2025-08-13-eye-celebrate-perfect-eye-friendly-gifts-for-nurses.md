Let’s face it—nurses do a lot of staring, and we’re not talking about watching reality TV. Night shifts, charting, and endless screen time can leave their eyes begging for a little TLC. If you're looking for gifts that show your favorite nurse you’ve got their backs—and their eyes—consider these fun and thoughtful options!

## 1. Blue Light Blockers: The Eyes Have It
The route to a nurse’s heart is through their... eyesight? Blue light glasses are a must-have for anyone glued to screens during marathon shifts. They help reduce eye strain and keep headaches at bay—all while making them look ultra-chic. After all, who said practical can’t be stylish?

## 2. Eye Masks: Spa Vibes, Minus the Reservation
When their shift ends, and it’s time for a power nap, a soothing eye mask is the perfect escape. Consider a plush, washable mask or even a cooling gel version for those extra intense nights. It's like a mini staycation for their eyes!

## 3. Personalized Eye Drops: Blink and You'll Miss It
Sure, eye drops might not be at the top of everyone’s wish list, but personalized options add a thoughtful twist. Throw in a dash of humor with a custom label for a nurse who knows the power of blinking—and hydration!

## 4. Task Lights: Let There Be Light
Desk or clip-on lights that mimic daylight can give those peepers a break. Ideal for charting in less-than-ideal lighting conditions, these are gifts your nurse will thank you for all year long.

## 5. Audiobooks: Give the Gift of Closing Their Eyes
Here's a gift that allows them to rest their eyes altogether! Subscriptions to an audiobook service mean they can listen to their favorite thrillers or cozy mysteries during commutes or winding down. It’s like hitting two birds with one headphone!

## Wrap It Up with a Wink
Whether it’s something cozy for their off hours or practical for their breaks, eye-friendly gifts show thoughtfulness and care.
