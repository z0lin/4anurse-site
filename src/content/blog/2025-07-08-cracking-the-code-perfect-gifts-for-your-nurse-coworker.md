Finding the perfect gift for your nurse coworker can be trickier than deciphering a doctor's handwriting. But fear not! With our witty gift guide, you'll score points and maybe even a shift swap!

## 1. Sweet Treats for Their Sweet Soul
When in doubt, bring out the snacks! Consider assembling a yummy care package featuring artisanal chocolates, gourmet popcorn, or an assortment of teas. It's a surefire way to bring some much-needed joy to those long shifts. After all, a well-fed nurse is a happy nurse!

## 2. Personalized Badge Reels
Nurses live for practicality but who says practical can't be stylish? Surprise your favorite Florence Nightingale with a cute, personalized badge reel. It'll add a dash of personality to their daily ensemble while keeping that ID close at hand.

## 3. Therapeutic Stress Buster
Helping others can be a bit stressful, to put it mildly. Help your coworker unwind with gifts like scented candles, a massage ball, or even a pocket-sized stress relief toy. They'll thank you when they're feeling Zen and not Zzzzz.

## 4. Humorous Tumblers
Inject some caffeine and comedy into their day with a funny tumbler. Whether it's holding their morning coffee or hydrating through the 2 a.m. yawns, a quirky mug can make all the difference. Plus, laughter is the best medicine, right?

## 5. Cozy Socks for Cuddly Comfort
If cozy socks don’t scream appreciation, then we don’t know what does. A pair of soft, snuggly socks will keep those hard-working feet happy as they clock in extra miles on the ward.
