Finding the perfect birthday gift for a nurse can be as tricky as nailing a perfect IV stick on the first try. But don’t fret! We’ve curated a list of gifts that'll make any nurse's day. Say goodbye to gifts that'll gather dust and hello to thoughtful presents they'll actually use.

## 1. Cozy Socks: Comfort Begins at the Feet
For those long 12-hour shifts, what's better than a pair of ultra-soft, cozy socks? Not only do they provide comfort, but they also add a hint of personality beneath those scrubs.

## 2. A Hilarious Tumbler: Hydration with Humor
Staying hydrated is paramount, and what better way than with a tumbler that offers a side of sass? Whether it’s coffee or water, a tumbler with a witty tagline will surely bring a smile to their face.

## 3. Badge Reels with Personality
Gone are the days of boring badge reels. Gift them one with flair—something cute or funny that'll complement their scrubs and show off their personality.

## 4. Aromatic Essential Oil Diffuser
Give the gift of relaxation with a portable essential oil diffuser. Perfect for unwinding after a hectic shift, these devices are a nurse’s aromatic escape to tranquility.

## 5. Planners: Keeping Chaos Organized
A planner helps manage the hectic schedule of a nurse. Opt for something stylish and functional, and watch them master their shifts like a pro.
