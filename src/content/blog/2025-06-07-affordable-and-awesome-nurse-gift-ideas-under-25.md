Who says you need to break the bank to show appreciation for the hardworking nurses in your life? We've compiled a list of delightful gifts that scream thoughtfulness, all under $25. Get ready to spread some joy without emptying your wallet!

 
## 1. Funky Badge Reels: A Touch of Personality
 Let's face it, badge reels are pretty much a nurse's best friend. They’re practical and easy to spruce up with quirky designs or funny message options. Give your favorite nurse a badge reel that brings a little personality (and maybe a chuckle) into their daily routine.

 
## 2. Cozy Socks: Warm and Fuzzy Feelings
 Nurses spend endless hours on their feet, so a pair of cozy socks is like heaven. Choose a pair that's soft, fun, and maybe even a bit silly. Trust us, their feet—and their heart—will thank you!

 
## 3. Whimsical Tumblers: Sip in Style
 Staying hydrated is crucial when working long shifts. A reusable tumbler with a whimsical design can brighten any nurse's day and keep drinks cool (or hot!) for those much-needed breaks.

 
## 4. Planner Pads: Keep It Organized
 We all know nurses juggle a million things at once, so why not make it a tad easier for them with a thoughtful planner pad? Look for ones with motivational quotes or uplifting designs to add that extra spark to their planning.

 
## 5. Scented Candles: Relaxation Station
 After hectic shifts, help your favorite nurse unwind with a scented candle that transports them to a place of zen. Opt for calming scents like lavender or eucalyptus for ultimate relaxation.
