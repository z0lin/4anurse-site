Bring the spa to their doorstep with essential oil-infused candle sets. Consider options that blend lavender for relaxation, eucalyptus to soothe, and a hint of citrus to uplift. These scents work like magic to melt stress away.

Have a nurse who’s all about that personal touch? Look for candle sets that allow customization of scents. With this personalized touch, they'll feel the love while basking in a peaceful glow.

## Cozy Vibes: Warm & Earthy Aromas
There’s something undeniably comforting about woodsy undertones. Candle sets with hints of cedarwood, amber, or vanilla can transform any room into a sanctuary of calm and coziness.

For the eco-conscious nurse, consider candles made from soy or beeswax with sustainable packaging. Little surprises like these show you care about their peace of mind and the planet.
