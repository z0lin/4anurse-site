## Snack Smart, Save Lives!
Let's face it: nurses are superheroes. But even superheroes need fuel to keep saving the day! Enter the healthy snack box—a gift idea packed with purpose, perfect for nurses always on the go. Whether they’re racing to the ER or delivering bedside care, nurses deserve snacks that are both nourishing and delicious.

### 1. Nuts About You!
Nothing says protein-packed like a sealed mix of nuts. From almonds to cashews, a selection of unsalted, roasted nuts can provide the crunchy boost any nurse needs between shifts. It’s brain food that’s easy to munch on while charting the day away.

### 2. Fruits (and Loose) for Thought
Consider including a selection of dried fruits, such as apricots, mango slices, or raisins. These bite-sized delights are like a fruity burst of energy when fresh options aren't available during a hectic day.

### 3. Dark Chocolate: The Indulgence They're Missing
Let’s sweeten the deal with some dark chocolate bites. Rich with antioxidants and just the right amount of sugar to offset a bitter coffee buzz, they fit perfectly in any pocket!

### 4. Snack Bars that Raise the Bar
Granola or protein bars are a no-brainer. They’re portable, mess-free, and, with the right selection, bursting with fiber and flavor. For any nurse catching a quick break, these bars are second to none.

### 5. Trailblazing Trail Mix
Trail mix offers a winning combo of nuts, seeds, and sometimes a cheeky bit of chocolate or coconut. This nutritious mix can be easily portioned, ensuring the nurse in your life has just the right amount through their shift.
