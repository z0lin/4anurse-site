Oops, you forgot that important date marked in bold on your calendar? Fear not! We're diving into the land of Amazon to discover the best last-minute gems that are sure to impress any nurse in your life. No need to panic-shop; you got this!

## Instant Relaxation with Cozy Socks
Nurses are on their feet more than a game of hopscotch, so give them the gift of plushy comfort with cozy socks. Opt for the ones with witty phrases or a cute design—bonus points if they have a non-slip grip for those tile floors at work.

## Bags of Fun—Literally!
Everyone loves a tote bag, especially nurses who need to carry their essentials like stethoscopes and secret candy stash. Grab a tote with a fun design or an inspiring quote for the ultimate utility-meets-personality gift!

## Mugs Full of Smiles
Let’s face it, coffee is basically a nurse’s BFF. A tumbler or mug with a funny or motivational quote could make those long morning shifts a lot more bearable—just fill to the brim with caffeine!

## Badge Reels with Personality
Badge reels are an everyday essential that also show off some personality. Look for ones featuring quirky characters or inspiring quotes to add a little flair to their scrubs.

## Take a Breather with Aromatherapy
Your favorite nurse deserves a nudge toward relaxation, and an aromatherapy kit is just the sneaky push they need. Oils with scents like lavender or eucalyptus are ideal for winding down after a shift.

Even if you’re scrambling for a last-minute gift, remember that it’s the thought and the laughs you can share that count the most.
