## When Scrubs Retire: A Toast to Incredible Careers
When it's time for a nurse to hang up their stethoscope for good, it's the perfect opportunity to honor their years of dedication with something memorable. No, we're not talking about a gold watch—think more heartfelt and personal. A retirement plaque or award speaks volumes, celebrating not just the date but the passion, hard work, and lives touched along the way.

### Why Plaques and Awards Rock
Retirement plaques and awards go beyond the traditional card. They provide a tangible reminder of a nurse’s career achievements—something proudly displayed on a wall or desk that screams *‘You’re a healthcare hero!’*

### Get Creative With Your Plaques
- **Personalized Messages**: Include a quote about compassion or nursing. It’s a sure-shot way to tug at their heartstrings.- **Photographic Touch**: Add a team photo or a snapshot from a memorable work event. Smiles every time, guaranteed.- **Milestone Markers**: Highlight years in service, departments worked in, or significant achievements. Talk about bragging rights!
### Awards with Personality
If you plan to put an award in their hand, make it as unique as the nurse themselves! From custom-engraved trophies to fun, themed awards that highlight their quirky side, the options are endless.

### Engraving Etiquette 101
When customizing texts, a dash of humor can make all the difference. For example, “To a Lifesaving Legend” or “Best in Bedside Manners” makes it personal and delightful.
