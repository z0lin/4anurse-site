## Feeling the Heat?
Summer delights—long days, sunshine, and that blissful school break. But for nurses, the season brings its own set of challenges. Whether they're clocking in extra hours or running between appointments, staying cool in every sense is a priority. Fear not, for we've gathered some breezy gift ideas to help your nurse beat the heat!

### Hydration Heroes
First on the list, hydration saves the day! Grab a double-insulated tumbler to keep drinks icy cold for hours on end. Bonus points if it comes with a witty healthcare pun!

### Cooling Comforts
Treat your nurse to a cooling towel. They work wonders by simply being wet and are perfect for placing around the neck during a break. Their compact design makes them an easy addition to any work bag.

### Light & Breezy
A breathable scrub cap does more than make a fashion statement. Opt for one that's lightweight and moisture-wicking to keep their head cool while they work hard under summer's blazing sun.

### Shady Business
Sunglasses aren't just for style points. Choose a pair with UV protection to safeguard those peepers during their precious moments outdoors. Choose lenses that offer anti-glare protection to add an extra layer of thoughtfulness.

### Fragrance-Free Skincare
Help them swap out greasy lotions with lightweight SPF-infused shields. The summer sun can be harsh, and wearing SPF is essential—even on those long night shifts.
