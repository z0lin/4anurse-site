So your daughter, son, niece, or best friend is graduating from nursing school. Congratulations — and welcome to the panic of trying to find a gift that isn't a generic "nurse" mug from Target.

Here's the good news: you don't have to know anything about medicine to give a thoughtful gift. You just need to think about what matters at this specific moment in their life.
