## Praise on a Plaque
Let’s face it—nurses are like glitter-infused superheroes, saving the day with every IV fluid bag they hang. They deserve a standing ovation, or at least, a personalized plaque shouting out their awesomeness. Whether it’s showcasing their name or memorializing a milestone, a personalized plaque is a thoughtful way to say ‘thank you’. 

### The Plaque Perks
**Custom Message:** From *‘You Make My Heart Skip a Beat!’* to *‘Best Shot Giver’*, the choice is yours! Add a personal touch with a quote that cracks a smile.

**Name & Title:** Picture it: their name shining under hospital lights, becoming the envy of the break room. It’s their badge of honor!

**Milestone Markers:** Celebrate their work anniversary or a significant accomplishment with a commemorative plaque. Twenty years of unparalleled dedication deserves more than lukewarm coffee.

### To Plaque or Not to Plaque?
Not sure if a plaque is the right choice? Ask yourself these questions: Does the nurse have an appreciation for humor, sentiment, or both? Do they have a dedicated space where they display personal achievements? If they’re nodding along, we have a winner!

### Perfect Plaque Spots
Aside from their locker door, this piece of appreciation can shine brightly on their desk, adorning a shelf, or vicariously hanging in their home office!
