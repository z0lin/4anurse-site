Is your mom a superhero in scrubs? Whether she's administering care or offering smiles, your nurse mom deserves something as thoughtful and special as she is. Let's dive into the world of DIY gifts that will make her say, 'You did this for me?!'

## 1. Handmade Aromatherapy Scrub Caps
If your mom's scrubs are her default attire, give her a stylish scrub cap infused with a hint of calm. Sew a cotton cap and slip a lavender sachet inside—it’s like a spa day, but make it mobile!

## 2. Personalized Tumbler with Quirky Quotes
Help her stay hydrated—because coffee and humor fuel her shifts! Customize a tumbler with a funny nursing quote, or even doodles from her favorite humans. It’s practical with a pinch of personality!

## 3. Cozy Knit Socks
Her shifts might be long, but her downtime should be cozy! Knit her a pair of warm socks—every step she takes at home will be a hug from you.

## 4. DIY Bath Soak Jar
Every mother deserves some pampering. Mix Epsom salt, baking soda, and her favorite essential oils in a mason jar. Add a ribbon and voilà—a mini escape she can enjoy after a tiring day!

## 5. Memory Journal with Your Touch
Get a plain journal and transform it with memories and inside jokes. Encourage her to jot down her thoughts, victories, and laughter-inducing stories from the hospital halls.

Whether your nurse mom is coming home from hero duty or gearing up for another day, these gifts are not just thoughtful—they're personalized creations that reveal how much heart you’ve put into her happiness.
