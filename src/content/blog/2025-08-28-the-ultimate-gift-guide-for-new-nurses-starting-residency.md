Welcome to the world of residency, where new nurses dive headfirst into their careers like a caffeinated marathon runner. It's a thrilling, rewarding, and sometimes exhausting ride that deserves a gift as bright as their future. So, let's spoil them with gifts that'll make their journey more joyful!

## First Things First: Comfort is Key
Your favorite new nurse will be running (literally) miles in their shoes, so why not start with a pair of cozy compression socks? They're like a hug for weary feet, reducing fatigue and swelling. Next up, consider an ultra-soft scrub jacket or an eye mask for those post-night-shift naps. Trust us, they'll thank you a thousand times over.

## Keep Energy Levels High with a Fun Tumbler
Every nurse knows the importance of a reliable tumbler to keep their coffee hot or their water cold through those long shifts. Choose one with a funny or inspiring quote to lighten those stressful days. It's the kind of caffeine companion that can become a shift favorite!

## Organizational Bliss with a Nurse Planner
Start them off on the right foot with a planner designed for busy healthcare professionals. These planners typically include sections for scheduling, patient notes, and wellness tips. Pair it with a set of colorful pens, and you've got the perfect duo for staying organized!

## Celebrate Their Hard Work with Some Nurse-Themed Jewelry
Add a little sparkle to their scrubs with a piece of nurse-themed jewelry. A subtle charm or a delicate bracelet will remind them of their achievements and the exciting road ahead.
