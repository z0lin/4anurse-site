## Waking Up to the Right Gift: Why Settle for Just One?
Let’s admit it, gift-giving is hard. Especially when the recipient is a superhero in scrubs! But fear not, for we have cracked the code: gift sets. These delightful bundles of joy are a one-way ticket to showing your appreciation without breaking the bank. We've scoured, and curated some delightful nurse gift sets under $50 that will have you crowned the hero of scrubs city!

### 1. Cozy Up in Style: The Relaxation Combo
The stresses of being a nurse can be washed away with a blissful spa day right at home. Look for a set that includes heavenly scented bath bombs, silky lotions, and perhaps a candle to add to the zen *so-glad-to-be-home* vibe.

### 2. Coffee & Tea Lovers Unite: Sip Sip Hooray!
If the nurse in your life runs on caffeine and good vibes, a coffee or tea sampler set is the perfect way to fuel their shifts. Include a fun, quirky tumbler, and they’ll think of you with every delightful sip.

### 3. Badge Reel Realness: Fashion Meets Function
Badge reels aren’t just a necessity; they’re a fashion statement too. Hunt for a gift set featuring colorful and delightful badge reels packed with personality. Bonus points if you can throw in a few matching pens or notepads to this charming combo.

### 4. First Aid for the Soul: Essential Oil Kits
A gift set showcasing soothing essential oils can be a fabulous stress-buster. Look for sets that offer a variety of calming scents, from lavender to eucalyptus, to uplift their spirits during off-duty hours.

### 5. Stay Cozy: Sock-tastic Starter Pack
Soft, funky socks are a must-have for nurses constantly on their feet. A gift set featuring multiple pairs of cute and cozy socks will warm their hearts (and toes).
